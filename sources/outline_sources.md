# Sources of the outline

Provenance of `OUTLINE.md`. **Never cited in printed text** (series rule: the
books do not name the programmes they follow). All URLs accessed 2026-10-22.
Several official hosts (education.gouv.fr, enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr,
planet-terre / planet-vie at ENS) refused or timed out. Their texts were
read from the mirrors listed.

## Astronomy and planetary strand

There is no French undergraduate Earth-science programme for this strand. Its
chapters follow the standard content of an introductory university astronomy
course (spherical astronomy, observation, planetary system, interstellar
medium and star formation, the Galaxy and galaxies, nucleosynthesis), as in
Karttunen et al., *Fundamental Astronomy* (Springer, 6th ed., 2017), table of
contents. Master-level course plans were checked only for scope: Grenoble
Alpes astrophysics track
(<https://formations.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/plugins/odf-web/odf/_content/subprogram-parcours-astrophysique_3/.pdf>)
and its interstellar-medium unit. Stellar structure, the HR diagram, Hubble's
law and the CMB are left to *One Physics Book* (b3 `astrophysics`,
`photons-phonons`).

## Overlap survey (other series)

All `parts/<year>/part.tex` files of `one-physics-book`, `one-biology-book`
and `one-chemistry-book` were listed, and every chapter matching an Earth or
space keyword was read at the level of its section and definition titles
(2026-10-22). The result is the cross-series table at the end of
`OUTLINE.md`. No existing book teaches plate tectonics, rocks and minerals,
stratigraphy and geological time, weather systems, ocean circulation,
landforms, palaeoclimate, or spherical trigonometry.

## Decisions made while drafting against the programmes

- *Weather and Climate* sits in grade 5, so grade 8
  (`winds-global-circulation`) explains the climate zones without
  introducing them a second time.
- *Latitude, Longitude and Time Zones* (g6) and *Maps of the Whole Earth*
  (g9) carry the physical-geography strand. No programme above owns them, but
  later chapters use them.
- *Hotspots and Intraplate Volcanism* (Y1) exists because ST-C-3 names
  intraplate volcanism and Y2 *Oceanic Islands* needs it.
- BG-C is split as the 2021 text splits it: Y1 takes the structure, energy
  budget and circulations; Y2 takes variability, archives, orbital forcing,
  deep time and anthropogenic change.
- Soils appear only as a geological recall (g10 `soil-formation`), since
  biology owns the soil profile at university.
- The fifth astronomy chapter is *Telescopes, Detectors and Photometry*
  (Y1), so that the exoplanet and galaxy chapters have an observational
  base.
