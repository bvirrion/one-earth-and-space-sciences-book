defined term of Book 5, then the terms of Spanish Books 1-4 that Book 5 uses (chapter `00`, matched wherever the English
form occurs). One line per group of English forms: `chapter | English | Spanish`. Use the Spanish form exactly, inflected
for number, gender and article.
