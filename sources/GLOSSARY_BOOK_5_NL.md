# Glossary: Earth and space Book 5 (`bachelor-3`) in Dutch

Written by the coordinator (`claude-opus-5-5`) on 2026-11-03 for the pilot run of the Dutch edition. It lists every defined
term of Book 5, then the terms of Dutch Books 1-4 that Book 5 uses (chapter `00`, matched wherever the English form occurs).
One line per group of English forms: `chapter | English | Dutch`. Use the Dutch form exactly, inflected for number, gender
and article. Dutch welds compounds: never write them apart.
